The Bake N Fry Narrative

A Cozy Spot Born from Passion, Melody & Fresh Dough

Nestled along Barrackpore Trunk Road (near Ramlal Agarwal Lane), Bake N Fry was created with a heartfelt vision: to provide Baranagar with a soulful café where good music, comfortable seating, and honest comfort food feel effortless.

Whether you are catching up with friends over our signature Chicken Cheese Sandwich, refueling after a workout with the wholesome Chicken Protein Bowl, or enjoying a slow solo coffee while listening to acoustic guitar melodies, we treat every dish as an opportunity to spark joy.

  • 100% Hygienic Kitchen Standards
  • Live Music Evenings
  • Vegan & Vegetarian Friendly
  • Free High-Speed Wi-Fi & Chill Seating
